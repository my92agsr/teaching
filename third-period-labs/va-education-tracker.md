## Governor Spanberger Administration

**Inaugurated:** January 2026

### Day One Executive Orders (January 17, 2026)
- Directs VDOE to strengthen core instruction in literacy and mathematics
- Establishes expert workgroups to improve Virginia Literacy Act implementation
- Accelerates access to advanced mathematics instruction
- Directs review of School Performance and Support Framework per 2025 JLARC report
- Development of next-generation assessment system
- Affirms rights and protections for students, parents, and teachers
- Emphasizes inclusive education regardless of background
- Launched cross-Commonwealth school listening tour (March 2026)

### Transition Team (Education)
- **Jim Dyke** - Former Secretary of Education (Gov. Wilder)
- **Anne Holton** - Former Secretary of Education (Gov. McAuliffe)

### Campaign Platform Highlights
- Modernize SOL exams
- Enforce student safety plans
- Increase disability support
- New literacy and math curriculums
- Recruit federal employees and servicemembers into teaching
- Expand statewide background checks for school staff
- Affordable early childhood education
- Investments in public colleges

## 2025-2026 Budget Highlights

### Major Investments
| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Direct Aid to Education (Biennium) | $22B+ | 54% increase over pre-COVID |
| Support Cap Elimination | $223M/year | Fully removes cap on support staff funding |
| English Learner Support | $110.7M increase | EL students tripled in 20 years (187k+ in 2024-25) |
| Special Education | $53M/year | New funding mechanism |
| Teacher/Staff Bonuses | One-time $1,000 | Plus 3% raise effective July 1, 2025 |
| Math Support | $12M | Pandemic learning loss recovery |
| Lab Schools (HBCU partnerships) | $25M | 15 K-12 Lab School models |

### Funding Challenges
- VDOE projects $1.2B needed over next two years
- House/Senate estimate baseline cost at $844M minimum
- Declining enrollment (birth rates, migration)
- JLARC: Local governments shouldering disproportionate share
- Virginia per-pupil funding below 50-state average
